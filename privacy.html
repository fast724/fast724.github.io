Privacy policy

Glass Home is a personal home screen used only on its owner's own TV.

What it accesses

  • With the owner's permission, the owner's Nest thermostat through Google's Smart Device Management (Device Access) API: temperature, humidity, mode and temperature setpoints, and changes to those settings made by the owner.
  • If enabled by the owner, room and home names shared through the same permission screen.

Where data goes

  • Thermostat data is requested directly from Google by the TV and shown on the TV. It is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or person.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the TV.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in Glass Home (Customize → Thermostat → Disconnect), in the Google Home app, or at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections.
